# Cheq-Us

**Discover. Research. Grow.**

Cheq-Us is an app idea that puts everything you need to know about a company in one place, so you don't have to search Google, open lots of websites and piece the information together yourself.

## Purpose

When people want to work for a company or apply for a job, they usually have to search Google and visit many different sites to find out who owns the company, what it does and whether it is hiring. Cheq-Us saves that time. You search once and find what you need right inside the app.

## What you can do

- **Check a company:** see the owner or CEO, when it started, its size, what it does, and the jobs it has open with their requirements.
- **Explore careers:** see what different careers involve and what you need to get started, for example the types of engineering jobs and the qualifications for each.
- **Find jobs:** browse openings, filter them by category and read the requirements before you apply.
- **Get job alerts:** choose the categories and location you care about and get notified when a company opens a new position.
- **Save items:** bookmark the companies and jobs you are interested in.
- **Search everything:** one search box for companies, jobs and careers.

## Why Cheq-Us

- One place instead of many websites
- Saves time and data
- Helps students and job seekers know what a job requires before applying
- Keeps everything inside the app, with no need to leave for other links

## Demo notice

This is a prototype. The companies and jobs shown are examples only, not live vacancies. A full version would use reliable public sources and show when each company's details were last checked.
